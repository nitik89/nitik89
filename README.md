#### 🏏 [Cricket Auction Room](https://github.com/nitik89/IPL-Auction-App1-main)
**[▶ Live](https://ipl-auction-room.onrender.com)**

#### 🪞 [AI Twin](https://github.com/nitik89/Ai-Twin)
**[▶ Live](https://ai-twin-navy.vercel.app)**

An AI that learns how you talk and think. It builds a profile of your tone and style, turns chats into long-term memories, and recalls them with vector search. Friends can chat with your twin, and a "mirror" shows you how it sees you.

`Next.js` `Gemini` `pgvector` `Prisma` `NextAuth`
